# The Lifetime Freedom Budget

# 人生自由账单

**A bilingual framework for calculating how much money you actually need to support the life you want.**

**一套用于计算“自己真正想过的人生到底需要多少钱”的双语长期财务框架。**

Created by **Chuanyan Liu / Elowen**

---

## Overview｜项目简介

Most people know how much they earn each month.

很多人知道自己一个月赚多少钱。

But far fewer people have seriously calculated:

但很少有人认真计算过：

> **How much money do I actually need for my entire life?**
> **我这一生，到底需要多少钱？**

The Lifetime Freedom Budget is a general life-planning framework designed to turn the vague idea of “financial freedom” into a personal, measurable model.

人生自由账单是一套通用的人生财务规划框架，用于把模糊的“财务自由”转化成一个属于个人、可以计算的模型。

It does not tell people how much they *should* spend.

它不告诉任何人“应该花多少钱”。

It does not define how much wealth is “enough” for everyone.

也不替所有人定义“多少钱才算够”。

Instead, it helps each person calculate:

它希望帮助每个人自己计算：

> **How much is enough to support the life I genuinely want to live?**

> **多少钱，足够支撑我真正想过的人生？**

---

# Why This Exists｜为什么做这个框架

Financial freedom is often discussed as if it were one universal number.

人们经常把财务自由讨论成一个统一数字。

But different people want very different lives.

但不同的人真正想要的人生完全不同。

One person may value travel.

有人重视旅行。

Another may value time.

有人更重视时间。

Someone may want to live in a major city.

有人喜欢住在大城市。

Someone else may prefer a quieter, lower-cost location.

也有人更喜欢安静、生活成本更低的地方。

Some people want to work for decades.

有人愿意长期工作。

Others may want to finish their period of high-intensity work much earlier.

也有人希望更早结束高强度工作阶段。

So instead of asking:

所以，与其问：

> “How much money should a person have?”

> “一个人应该有多少钱？”

This framework asks:

这套框架问的是：

> **“What does my life actually cost?”**

> **“我真正想过的人生，到底需要多少钱？”**

# The Framework｜框架结构

The model is divided into fifteen major components.

整个模型主要分为十五个部分。

---

## 01. Basic Living Costs

## 基础生活成本

Calculate the monthly cost of maintaining your current life.

先计算维持当前生活方式每个月真正需要多少钱。

Possible categories include:

可以包括：

* Housing｜住房
* Food｜饮食
* Transportation｜交通
* Personal consumption｜个人消费
* Healthcare｜医疗与健康
* Utilities｜水电网及通信
* Insurance｜保险
* Daily necessities｜日常用品

### Formula

**Basic Monthly Cost = Total Essential Monthly Expenses**

**基础月成本 = 所有必要月支出之和**

---

## 02. Desired Lifestyle

## 真正想过的生活

Add the things that make life personally meaningful or enjoyable.

加入真正让自己觉得生活值得的部分。

Examples:

例如：

* Travel｜旅行
* Hobbies｜兴趣
* Entertainment｜娱乐
* Quality-of-life purchases｜品质消费
* Collections｜收藏
* Personal services｜生活服务
* Time-saving services｜购买时间的服务

The goal is not to judge these expenses.

这里不是为了判断这些消费“值不值得”。

The goal is to measure them honestly.

而是诚实地计算它们。

---

## 03. Irregular but Predictable Expenses

## 不按月发生、但长期一定会发生的费用

Many budgets underestimate real costs because they only track monthly expenses.

很多预算失真，是因为只计算每月固定账单。

Examples include:

例如：

* Electronics replacement｜电子设备更新
* Furniture｜家具
* Appliances｜家电
* Moving｜搬家
* Vehicle replacement｜车辆更换
* Repairs｜维修
* Medical expenses｜医疗支出
* Legal / accounting services｜法律及会计服务
* Administrative costs｜行政及证件费用

### Formula

**Annual Irregular Expenses ÷ 12 = Monthly Equivalent Cost**

**年度不定期支出 ÷ 12 = 月均摊成本**

---

## 04. Personal vs. Family Responsibilities

## 个人生活与家庭责任分开计算

Separate your own financial needs from optional or future responsibilities.

把自己的生活需求，与其他家庭责任分开计算。

Possible modules:

可单独建立：

* Self｜自己
* Partner｜伴侣
* Children｜子女
* Parents / family｜父母或其他家庭成员
* Pets｜宠物

This keeps the model flexible as life changes.

这样即使人生结构发生变化，也不需要推翻整个模型。

---

## 05. Existing Debt

## 历史债务

Past obligations should be separated from future lifestyle costs.

过去的债务，与未来的生活成本应该分开。

Examples:

例如：

* Student loans｜学生贷款
* Credit-card debt｜信用卡债务
* Personal loans｜个人借款
* Medical debt｜医疗欠款
* Tax obligations｜税务欠款

### Formula

**Total Existing Debt = One-Time Cleanup Requirement**

**历史债务总额 = 一次性清账金额**

---

## 06. Emergency Reserve

## 安全现金池

Ask:

先问自己：

> **If my income stopped tomorrow, how long would I want to remain financially independent?**

> **如果明天收入突然停止，我希望多久以内依然不需要因为钱做违背意愿的决定？**

Possible targets:

可以选择：

* 3 months
* 6 months
* 12 months
* 24 months

### Formula

**Emergency Reserve = Essential Monthly Cost × Safe Months**

**安全现金池 = 必要月生活成本 × 无收入安全月数**

Additional reserves may include:

还可以加入：

* Medical emergencies｜医疗
* Relocation｜搬迁
* Legal needs｜法律
* Career transitions｜职业转换
* Entrepreneurship risk｜创业风险
* Family emergencies｜家庭突发事件

---

## 07. Three Lifestyle Levels

## 三档生活版本

不要只计算一个数字，至少建立三个版本。

### B. Comfortable Level｜舒适线

不需要频繁因为小额支出做取舍。

The lifestyle you genuinely prefer.

按照自己真正喜欢的方式安排生活。

## 08. Geographic Variables

## 地理变量

Compare:

**提高收入是一种增加自由的方法。**

**降低同一种理想生活的实现成本，也是。**

## 09. Planning Horizon

选择希望规划的未来时间长度。

Examples:

例如：

* 20 years

### Static Formula

**Annual Living Cost × Planning Years**

**年生活成本 × 规划年数**

## 10. Inflation

## 通胀

Today's money will not necessarily purchase the same lifestyle decades from now.

今天的钱，几十年以后不一定还能购买同样的生活。

Possible scenarios:

可以建立：

* 0% — Static model｜静态模型
* 2% — Lower inflation assumption｜较低通胀
* 3% — Moderate assumption｜中等假设
* Higher-rate stress test｜更高通胀压力测试

The purpose is not perfect prediction.

目的不是预测得绝对准确。

It is to avoid pretending future prices will remain unchanged.

而是避免默认未来几十年的价格永远不变。

## 投资与资产收益

一生的支出并不一定全部依赖劳动收入。

Possible scenarios:

可以分别计算：

### Scenario 1 — No Investment Returns

### 情景一｜完全不计算投资收益

### Scenario 2 — Conservative Returns

### 情景二｜保守收益

### Scenario 3 — Moderate Returns

### 情景三｜中性收益

### Scenario 4 — High-Risk Assets / Entrepreneurship

### 情景四｜高风险资产 / 创业

对于高风险情景，同时计算：

**Success + Normal + Failure**

**成功 + 普通 + 失败**

Do not model only the best outcome.

不要只计算最漂亮的结果。

> “这是多少钱？”

还可以问：

> **How much of my life does this money buy back?**

这样财富就不再只是账户里的抽象数字。

# Core Formula｜核心公式

## Lifetime Freedom Gap

## 人生自由缺口

```text
Future Lifestyle Needs
+ Existing Obligations
+ Safety Buffer
- Current Available Net Assets
- Reasonably Expected Future Asset Returns
- Other Reliable Future Income
= Lifetime Freedom Gap
```

```text
未来生活需求
+ 历史负担
+ 安全缓冲
- 当前可用净资产
- 可合理预计的未来资产收益
- 其他确定性未来收入
= 人生自由缺口
```

Then:

然后：

```text
Lifetime Freedom Gap
÷ Years You Are Willing to Continue High-Intensity Work
= Required Annual Wealth Creation and Retention
```

```text
人生自由缺口
÷ 愿意继续高强度工作的年数
= 每年真正需要创造并留下的财富
```

# Philosophy｜核心理念

Financial freedom should not begin with:

财务自由不应该从这个问题开始：

> **“How much money do other people have?”**

> **“别人有多少钱？”**

It should begin with:

而应该从：

> **“What life do I actually want?”**

> **“我真正想过什么样的人生？”**

And then:

然后再问：

> **“How much is enough to support it?”**

> **“多少钱足够支撑它？”**

Your freedom number does not need to look like anyone else's.

你的自由数字不需要和任何人一样。
